7The war under the war — money, power, ideology
The thesis: Israel wants Iran broken the way Syria broke. The arms makers, the oil exporters and the dollar-first hawks are happy to help. Russia profits. China stays quiet and likes America tied down. The only brake is the American voter — and it is tightening fast.
The case against: Israel pays nothing today, but its planners still fear a long Gulf war that drains the US interceptors it depends on; China loses on $97 oil more than it gains on discounted Iranian crude; Russia's windfall is gross, not net — Ukrainian drones have cut ~40% of its refining; and lobbies have outlived hostile polls for decades.

Who wants this war to keep going

Right = gains from a long war. Left = pays for it. The winners sit closest to Washington.

Washington: engine vs. brake

Engine = Israel lobby + arms industry + oil exporters + dollar hawks. Brake = voters, gas price, Trump's approval, anti-war Republicans. The gap is the direction of the war.
